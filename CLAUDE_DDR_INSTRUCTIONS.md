## Design decision governance

- Read `docs/decisions/README.md` and `docs/decisions/INDEX.md` before proposing or implementing material game-mechanics changes.
- Draft each material game-design decision as a new `docs/decisions/DDR-NNNN-short-title.md` using `DDR-TEMPLATE.md`.
- All new DDRs begin as **Proposed**. Do not record approval without explicit project-owner confirmation.
- Accepted DDRs control the design of their specific mechanics; keep the master design consistent with them.
- Do not silently reconcile conflicting design documents, DDRs, and code. Report the conflict for a decision.
- Never modify or delete prior DDR history; supersede an older decision with a new DDR.
- When implementation is authorized, link code and tests to the accepted DDR and update its implementation mapping with verified facts.
- Keep `docs/decisions/INDEX.md` current.
